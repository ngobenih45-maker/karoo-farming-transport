Our story

Built on the road between the Cape and the north

Karoo Farming Transport operates from Mieliefontein Farm, just off the N1 Highway at Hanover — the main artery connecting Cape Town, Bloemfontein and Johannesburg.

Our location is a deliberate advantage. From here our trucks reach the Cape, the Free State, Gauteng and beyond without detours, and our dispatch desk is open round the clock so your inquiry or booking is never left waiting.

We move general cargo, building materials, groceries, furniture, farm produce and heavy bulk loads for businesses and households across South Africa — with the same standards on a 1-ton delivery as on a 32-ton interlink.
