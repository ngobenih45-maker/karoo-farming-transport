About us

Rooted in the Karoo. Moving the nation.

Karoo Farming Transport is a freight and logistics company based off the N1 Highway at Hanover — perfectly placed on South Africa’s main north–south freight corridor.

We combine a modern fleet with a simple promise: your cargo arrives safe, reliable and on time. Every load is handled by licensed, insured drivers, with clear communication from the first call to final delivery.

  • On-time delivery, every time
  • Affordable rates with no hidden costs
  • Tracking and regular updates
